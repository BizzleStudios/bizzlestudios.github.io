Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in Beta?
Beta includes basic material fixing with 10 materials per fix, Built-in ↔ URP conversion, and one-click fix. It's a limited version to test the core features.
When will Lite be released?
Lite is planned for Late September - Early October 2026 at $11.99. It will include unlimited materials, batch processing, and custom rules.
Can I upgrade from Beta to Lite?
Yes! Beta users get exclusive pricing: upgrade to Lite for only $4.99 (58% OFF). First 100 users only.
Will HDRP be supported?
HDRP support is coming in Standard v1.5 (December 2026). Beta and Lite versions focus on Built-in ↔ URP conversion.
Do I get free updates?
Yes! All version updates within your tier are included. Major version upgrades (e.g., Lite → Standard) require separate purchase.
What Unity versions are supported?
Unity 2020.3 and newer. Tested on Unity 2022.3 LTS. HDRP support requires Unity 2021.3+.
